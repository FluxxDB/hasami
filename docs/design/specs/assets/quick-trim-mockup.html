Small progress window

Opens for right-click Compress and Convert, then closes itself when done. It shows the size going in, the target, and the resolution Hasami picked to stay under 20 MB.

Where the red goes

Still only three places: the playhead, the Quick export border, and the "edited" dot. It's now a duller red that doesn't fight the audio colors. A selected clip still gets a bone-white outline.

Type

Small text is uppercase and letter-spaced, like gallery signage. The serif italic appears only in the wordmark ("quick trim", "compress"). Fonts here are free stand-ins; the doc's Kmr Waldenburg and Editorial New are paid faces.

No chrome

No gradients, no filled buttons, no glows. Buttons are hairline-bordered and marked with an arrow. The video sits on pure black so the frame is the brightest thing on screen.
